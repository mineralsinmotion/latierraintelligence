Community Engagement

The Medano Earth Collective

The Medano Earth Collective is a community engagement and education initiative of La Tierra Resource Intelligence LLC, based in Medanales, New Mexico, along the Chama River. Its work connects Earth science with everyday life and the ways communities understand and use local resources.

Local Earth & Water

Exploring the Chama River, acequia systems, groundwater, regional geology, land use, and how residents interact with natural resources. Activities may include field observations, historical research, mapping, and community learning.

GIS Education

Introduction to Geographic Information Systems (GIS), digital mapping, spatial data, coordinate systems, and practical mapping exercises using local and other topics. Contact La Tierra to inquire about instruction.

Earth Science Sunday School

Summer 2027. An informal educational program exploring geology, minerals, hydrology, Earth processes, and northern New Mexico landscapes through observation, discussion, and hands-on activities.
