Data, Spreadsheet & Database Services

La Tierra Resource Intelligence assists individuals, businesses, and organizations with creating, organizing, and managing spreadsheets, databases, and other information resources.

Small, one-time projects are welcome. Services are scoped to the client’s needs.

Spreadsheets & Excel

Creation, modification, and organization of spreadsheets for records, inventories, projects, and other information. Assistance can include formulas, formatting, and tracking tables.

Database Design & Development

Relational database design, table and relationship organization, migration from spreadsheets, and SQL queries according to project requirements.

Document Conversion & Data Organization

PDF-to-Excel table conversion, CSV conversions, record consolidation, and data cleanup. Results depend on the quality and format of source documents.

Reporting & Practical Instruction

Summaries, charts, and reports based on information provided by the client. Individual instruction in Excel and practical information management is also available.
