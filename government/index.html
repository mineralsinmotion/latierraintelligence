Government Contracting

La Tierra Resource Intelligence LLC provides independent geoscience research, scientific data services, and technical information compilation for government agencies, research organizations, and industry. Our work integrates historical and contemporary Earth science, geological, mineral-resource, and critical-mineral information into documented technical and geospatial products.

Core Competencies

  • Geological, mineral-resource, and critical-minerals research
  • Global mineral-industry research and country-level information reporting
  • Historical and contemporary scientific data compilation, migration, reconciliation, and analysis
  • Geographic information systems (GIS), cartography, and spatial analysis
  • Groundwater, hydrology, and environmental data research
  • Primary-source literature, archival research, and technical reporting
  • Geological field investigations and mineralogical and petrographic data interpretation

Relevant Expertise

La Tierra's principal brings professional experience in global mineral-resource research and international country reporting; geological and mineral research, including microbeam instrumentation and geochemical analysis; emergency-response data analytics and data science; environmental cleanup and chromium-plume groundwater oversight with federal Contracting Officer's Representative II responsibilities; and state oil and gas compliance and regulatory review.

Professional geological field and research work spans North and South America, Asia, and Oceania. Language skills include Spanish and Japanese.

This experience is attributable to the principal individually and is not presented as prior contract performance by La Tierra Resource Intelligence LLC.
